# Publication claim ledger

All performance numbers below come from frozen Cycle018/022 receipts. Provenance findings come from the Cycle023 audit and historical log excerpts. No new performance estimate was computed.

| claim | evidence | allowed wording |
|---|---|---|
| Causal identity-memory switching: supported as an input intervention on these evaluated groups | Cycle022/scoring/comparison.json summaries: degraded CMSA 30/50 versus 1/50; Cycle022 prediction/read audits hold image and semantic query fixed across identities | Changing supplied identity memory can switch the relational target; frozen CMA satisfies the strict two-identity criterion more often on these evaluated groups. This does not isolate the causal benefit of any individual architecture component or establish unseen-image generalization. |
| Absolute degraded performance: supported within fixed stressor | Cycle022/scoring/comparison.json: target15_b mIoU 68.02% versus 27.52%; Fidelity 87% versus 38%; IER 2% versus 30%. Training exposure and historical selection limit external generalization. |

No result against RAS or RegionReasoner is established here. |

Primary allowed wording: **On these evaluated counterfactual groups with supplied miner identity memories and reconstructed pseudo-label targets, frozen CMA base-w15 achieves higher absolute target15_b segmentation accuracy and identity-memory fidelity than the pinned released SegLLM system. The reconstructed historical training protocol overlaps both evaluated splits, and the source holdout participated in historical checkpoint comparisons; these results do not establish training-unseen generalization or architecture-only superiority.**
